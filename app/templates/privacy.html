Data and privacy

Demo examples are fictional. The app makes no AI calls and uses no analytics or database. A random, HttpOnly session cookie identifies your current studio.

Imported JSON stays in server memory, isolated to your session, and is removed after 30 minutes without activity. Clear session removes it immediately. Restarting the server removes all sessions. Exports remain on your device until you delete them.

No studio content is backed up in browser storage. Only your light/dark theme preference is stored there. Do not import sensitive personal data, confidential briefs, or material you do not have permission to use.

The supplied server command disables access logs. Hosting infrastructure may process request metadata. Live research, moderation and private access controls are not implemented yet.
